How the counter works

Tick spreads your pay across the time you actually work. Count only during your working hours and a day's pay is divided across those hours; count around the clock and it is divided across all 24.

Gross and take-home are shown together on purpose. The gap between them is the part you never see, and the Tax tab breaks it down.

Common questions

The take-home figure does not match my payslip

Your employer works tax out from allowances you have declared to them, which never appear on a payslip, so no estimate can be exact. Turn on Settings, Use my payslip's tax and enter the monthly figure from your payslip. Tick uses that instead of estimating.

I am not in Cyprus

Settings, Tax, Manage templates. Duplicate a built-in to get an editable copy, then set the bands, the ceilings and the caps for your country. The whole app follows it, including what your health contribution is called.

My tax has bands, not one percentage

That is what templates are for. Flat rate applies a single percentage; any other template applies progressive bands the way a tax table reads.

The counter is not moving

Check Settings, Schedule. Outside your working hours, or on a day that is not counted, it holds still. Count around the clock makes it run continuously.

My running total resets on the wrong day

Settings, Counter resets: the end of the month, a fixed day, or the last working day of the month.

This week looks bigger than this month

It should not be. Both are clamped to your Counting starts date in Settings, Dates. If that date is wrong, everything downstream is.

I want my career total to be real, not estimated

Summary, then Pay history. Add each month from its payslip. Recorded months are never recalculated, so a later raise does not rewrite your past.

The widget is out of date

It refreshes on a budget iOS controls, so it can lag by a few seconds. Opening and leaving the app pushes it an update.

I want a different language

Settings, Appearance, Language. English, Greek, Turkish, Russian, German, Romanian and Hebrew, independent of your phone's setting.

It is not syncing between my devices

There is no sync. Everything stays on the device you entered it on.

I want to start over

Settings, Reset defaults.
