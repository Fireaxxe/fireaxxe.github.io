Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 9 November 2026

Thank you for choosing Shrink. This policy explains how your data is handled when you use the app. The short version: it never leaves your device.

1. No Data Collection

Shrink does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics.

2. No Network Access

Shrink has no networking code of any kind. Your photos, videos and audio cannot be uploaded, because the app has no means of uploading anything.

3. How the App Works

  • All compression runs locally on your device, using Apple's own media frameworks.
  • Files you choose are read only to compress them, and the result is written only where you ask for it to be saved.
  • Shrink removes location and camera metadata from the files it produces.
  • A small amount of preference data — such as whether you have seen the welcome screen — is stored only on your device.

4. Photos Access

If you grant access to your photo library, it is used only to read the file you pick and to save the compressed copy back when you ask. Shrink does not browse, index, or read anything you have not selected.

5. No Third-Party Tracking

Shrink contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. Nothing in the app reports on how you use it.

6. Children's Privacy

Because Shrink collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated. If it is, the new version will appear here with an updated “Effective Date.”
