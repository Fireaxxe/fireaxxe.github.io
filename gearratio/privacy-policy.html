GearRatio – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 11/Nov/2026

Last Updated: 11/Nov/2026

1. The short version

GearRatio does not collect anything. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything you type stays on your iPhone.

2. What the app stores, and where

The app stores what you enter about your vehicles: the tyre sizes you compare, any measurements you take of your own tyres, your gearbox and transfer case ratios, your differential ratio and axle, your engine's redline, and the names of the vehicles you save. It also stores your preferences, which are the unit system, how much tyre squat to assume, the app's language, the tyre sizes you have typed recently, and any axles you have added yourself.

All of it is written to a private container on your own device, shared only between the GearRatio app and its own widget so that the widget can show the same figures. No other app can read it.

If you use iCloud Backup, your device backup may include this data, the same way it includes data from other apps. That backup is between you and Apple. GearRatio does not send anything to iCloud itself.

3. What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, or sell any data.
  • It does not track you, and it does not use any advertising or analytics services.
  • It contains no third-party SDKs.
  • It does not use the camera, the microphone, your location, your contacts, or your photo library.

4. The one time the app uses the network

GearRatio has no server of its own and makes no network requests of its own. The only network traffic it causes is Apple's: when you open the purchase screen, buy GearRatio Pro, or restore a previous purchase, the app asks the App Store for the price and for whether you already own it.

That conversation is between your device and Apple, using Apple's own StoreKit. Your payment details are handled entirely by Apple and are never seen by this app or by its developer. The app learns only one thing from it: whether this Apple Account owns the unlock.

5. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing, from anyone.

6. Deleting your data

Removing a vehicle in the app deletes that vehicle's record. "Erase everything" in Settings deletes every saved vehicle, your recent sizes, and the comparison on screen. Deleting the app removes everything the app has stored. Your Pro unlock is not affected by any of this, because it belongs to your Apple Account rather than to the app's data.

7. Accuracy

GearRatio is a calculator and a reference. The differential ratios it lists as available for each axle come from gear manufacturers' and suppliers' catalogues and change over time, so they are a strong default rather than a guarantee. Confirm with whoever is selling you the parts before ordering.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy in the future. Any changes will be reflected in this document and the date above will change with it.
