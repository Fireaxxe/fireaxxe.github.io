Dog Years – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 03/Nov/2026

Last Updated: 03/Nov/2026

1. The short version

Dog Years does not collect anything. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no network code in the app at all. Everything you type stays on your iPhone.

2. What the app stores, and where

The app stores what you enter about your dogs: name, date of birth, an optional adoption date, size, an optional photo, and any weights you log. It also stores your display preferences, which are the conversion method and whether weights show in kilograms or pounds.

All of it is written to a private container on your own device, shared only between the Dog Years app and its own widget so that the widget can show the same figures. No other app can read it.

If you use iCloud Backup, your device backup may include this data, the same way it includes data from other apps. That backup is between you and Apple. Dog Years does not send anything to iCloud itself.

3. What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, or sell any data.
  • It does not track you, and it does not use any advertising or analytics services.
  • It contains no third-party SDKs.
  • It makes no network requests of any kind.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules birthday reminders locally on your device. They are created and delivered by iOS itself. Nothing is sent to a server, and no push notification service is involved.

5. Photos

If you choose a photo for a dog, a copy is saved inside the app's own private container so the widget can show it. The app does not read your photo library beyond the single image you pick, and the copy is deleted when you remove the photo or the dog.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing, from anyone.

7. Deleting your data

Removing a dog in the app deletes that dog's record and any photo saved for it. Deleting the app removes everything the app has stored.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy in the future. Any changes will be reflected in this document and the date above will change with it.
